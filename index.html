Production facilities

    Concrete and joint works

      Concrete paving

      Our Wirtgen SP1600 slipform paver places airfield and highway concrete in one continuous pass — spreading, vibrating and finishing the slab. For smaller areas and tight spots we use fixed-form pavers and manual paving.

      Joint sawing

      Our 14 hydraulic-drive concrete saws cut joints up to 50 cm deep, including horizontal cuts. We make the diamond blades for them ourselves.

      50 cmcutting depth with Ø1200 mm blades

      Megaf Drill

      Our drilling rig for dowel bars and tie bars. It drives on its tracks along the slab edge, on top of the concrete. A red hydraulic cylinder swings its three yellow arms down 90° once; with the arms down, the rock drills bore horizontally under the machine into the slab edge — three holes at once — then the machine moves on and drills again without raising the arms. With its tracked chassis, diesel engine and on-board air compressor, it is self-contained and moves across the site on its own.

      3drilling heads at once

      Concrete form installation machine

      Our own design for placing side forms ahead of the paver. The machine takes each loose form in at the front, pushes it tight against the form already fixed, and holds it on line and level inside its tunnel. Two steering sensors at the front and rear follow the stringline for direction, and the middle sensor sets the height. When it stops, it drills the base and drives the fixing pin automatically, fed one by one from the magazine; as it moves on, the forms are oiled on the concrete side.

      300–500mm form height, adjustable tunnel

      L-450 side form

      Our own production: the steel side form placed by our form installation machine. It is 2300 mm long, made of 8 mm plate, with a 250 mm base. The upper L section bolted on the machine side sets the grade: with it down the form is 45 cm high, raised it is 50 cm, and the concrete face stays flat. Each form has four Ø24 holes at an equal 575 mm pitch, 125 mm from the concrete face; a Ø20 pin is driven into the base through each hole to fix the form.

      45–50 cmadjustable form height

      Old concrete breaking

      Renewing a runway or road starts with removing the old slabs. Our tracked breaker lifts a heavy hammer and drops it onto the concrete, fracturing the slab in place so it can be removed quickly.
